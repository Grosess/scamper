How to play

Don't get caught, or catch everyone.

Mode

Classic tag

One player is IT. Tag someone to pass it on, and they can't tag you straight back. The player who spends the least time as IT wins.

Mode

Infection

Tagged players join the seekers. Runners win if anyone is still free when time runs out.

Mode

Duel (1v1)

Exactly two players. One chases, one evades, and each chase is on a short timer. Tag them and you score; survive and they score. Whoever wins a chase evades next. First to 10 (or whatever the host picks) wins.

The void

Every map floats in a black void. Fall off during a round and you come back as a seeker: IT in Classic, infected in Infection, and in a Duel your opponent scores.

Maps
    Level features
    • Jump pads (glowing rings) launch you up onto high spots.
    • Water slides (striped ramps) make you slide no matter what.
    • Bouncy stuff: ball pits, lifeboats and pool trampolines throw you back up.
    Movement
    • Slide while running for a burst of speed. Sliding downhill on a ramp builds even more.
    • Jump out of a slide to keep your speed. Hold slide as you land to chain another one.
    • Wall kick: jump next to a wall while in the air. You'll launch toward where you're looking.
    • Kick back and forth between two walls to climb. You can't kick the same wall twice in a row.
    • Mantle: push into a ledge in mid-air to pull yourself up.
    • To tag, just run into someone. When you become IT you get a short cooldown before you can tag, and in Classic you can't tag straight back.
    • Seekers run a little faster than runners.
