    How to play

    Mode

    Bomb Tag

    One player gets the bomb. Run into someone to pass it on (no instant pass-backs). When the fuse runs out, whoever holds it is out and a new bomb drops. Last one standing wins. The bomb holder runs a little faster.

    Mode

    Freeze Tag

    Seekers (about one per four players) freeze runners by tagging them. Run into a frozen teammate to thaw them - they're safe for a moment after. Seekers win by freezing everyone at once; runners win if the clock runs out.

    Mode

    King of the Tag

    Whoever wears the crown scores a point every second. Run into the king to steal it (a new king is safe for 2 seconds). Fall off and the crown drops in the middle. Most points wins.

    Mode

    Duel (1v1)

    Two players take turns chasing. Catch the runner before the timer ends to score; survive and the runner scores. The chaser sees the runner's outline through walls. First to the target wins.

    Mode

    Race

    Everyone starts together on a race course. Get to the finish first. There are no checkpoints: fall off and you're back at the start. The leaderboard shows how far everyone has to go.

    Mode

    Grand Prix

    A run of 3, 5 or 7 rounds. Each round is a random mode on a random map. Placings score points (10, 7, 5, 4, 3, 2, then 1 each; Freeze Tag gives 8 to the winning side and 3 to the rest). Most points after the last round wins.

    Modifier

    Randomizer

    Turn it on for any mode. At random moments something happens to everyone - and sometimes two things at once. There are 25: No Gravity, Tiny, Lights Out, Zoomies, Ice Rink, Moon Shoes, Slow Mo, Upside Down, Mirror Mode, Backwards, Woozy, Tornado, Disco, 8-Bit, Fisheye, Invisible, Big Heads, Impostors, X-Ray, Windy, Bouncy Floors, Rocket Jump, Earthquake, Red Light Green Light, Swap! and Teleport.

    Maps
      Movement
      • Slide while running for a burst of speed. Jump out of a slide to keep it.
      • Wall kick: jump next to a wall while in the air - you launch toward where you look. Kick again and again to climb - the same wall or back and forth between two. Orange walls mark kick routes.
      • Mantle: push into a ledge in mid-air to pull yourself up.
      • Squeezing under something low doesn't slow you down.
      • Jump pads launch you up. Some platforms move - ride them, and watch out for spinning bars.
      • To tag, just run into someone.

        Privacy policy

        Last updated October 30, 2026

        Scamper is a free browser game. We collect as little as possible, and we never sell or share it.

        Playing as a guest

        No account, nothing stored by us. Your settings and stats stay in this browser (local storage). You get a random guest name like "bobo".

        If you make an account

        We store only:

        • your username (pick one that isn't your real name)
        • your password as a salted scrypt hash - never the password itself, and nobody, including us, can read it
        • your game settings and stats
        • the colors you've won
        • when the account was made and last saved

        We don't ask for or keep an email, real name, age, birthday, school, location, photos or contacts. No ads, no analytics, no tracking cookies.

        How it's used

        Only to run the game: log you in, keep your stuff on any computer, hand out prize colors, and let the host of your game check that your name and colors are really yours (they get your username and colors, nothing else).

        Playing online

        Games connect players' browsers directly (WebRTC, matched up through the free PeerJS server). Like any direct connection, the other players' browsers can see your network (IP) address. Other players see your username, look and movements while you play.

        Who stores it

        The game is hosted on Vercel and GitHub Pages, and account data is kept in an Upstash database. These services keep their own normal server logs (for example IP addresses) for security.

        Keeping it safe

        Everything travels over HTTPS. Logins are rate-limited against guessing. A login lasts 60 days on a device, or until you log out.

        Deleting it

        Account → "Delete my account" removes your account and everything stored with it, right away and for good. Logging out removes it from this computer. Clearing your browser data removes guest settings and stats.

        Kids

        Scamper is made for students. Because we don't collect personal information, please don't put your real name or anything personal in your username.

        Questions or changes

        Questions: open an issue at github.com/Grosess/scamper. If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too.
